Dealer FAQs
How do I know my dealer tier?
Your dealer tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum) is shown on your profile page and price list. Tiers are based on total business volume with Raj Marketing.
What are dealer discounts?
Bronze: 15% OFF · Silver: 20% OFF · Gold: 25% OFF · Platinum: 30% OFF. Discounts apply to retail prices shown in the price list.
How do I get bulk order discounts?
Additional bulk discounts apply: 200+ sq.ft → +5%, 500+ sq.ft → +10%, 1000+ sq.ft → +15% off.
What is the payment schedule?
Standard dealer terms: 60% advance on order confirmation, 40% balance on delivery. Talk to us for custom terms based on volume.
What is the delivery time?
Standard: 3-5 working days for regular orders. Custom/bulk orders may take longer. Delivery within Mysore is free.
Do you provide marketing support?
Yes! We provide marketing materials, product catalogues, and co-branded promotional support. Contact us for details.
