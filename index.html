Frequently Asked Questions

Best net for sliding doors?
Sliding mosquito nets or aluminium frame nets are ideal for sliding doors.
Best net for windows?
Velcro, aluminium frame, and pleated nets all work well for windows.
Velcro vs Aluminium Frame?
Velcro is adhesive-based and flexible. Aluminium frames are permanent and durable.
Are Pleated Nets good for large doors?
Yes, pleated nets are designed for large openings and fold away neatly.
Do you provide measurement?
Yes, professional on-site measurement. Free within Mysore.
Do you provide installation?
Yes, professional installation for all our mosquito net systems.
How long does installation take?
Typically 1-3 hours depending on the number of windows/doors.
What mesh options are available?
Stainless steel (SS) and fibre (Touflex) mesh in various grades.
What is the warranty?
SS Mesh & Pleated: 1-year. Aluminium Frame: 5-year against defects.
Do you serve outside Mysore?
Yes, we serve Mysore, Mandya, Chamrajanagar, Gundlupet, H.D. Kote, Kollegala and nearby.
How do I get an estimate?
Use our online estimate form, AI price checker, or contact us directly.
Can I send a photo on WhatsApp?
Yes, send photos to our WhatsApp number for guidance and a preliminary estimate.
