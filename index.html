Frequently Asked Questions

What mosquito net is best for sliding doors?
Sliding mosquito nets or aluminium frame nets are ideal for sliding doors. They provide easy operation and a tight seal.
What mosquito net is best for windows?
Velcro nets, aluminium frame nets, and pleated nets all work well for windows depending on your space and budget.
What is the difference between Velcro and Aluminium Frame?
Velcro nets are adhesive-based, flexible and ideal for temporary or renters. Aluminium frames are permanent, durable and provide a premium finish.
Are Pleated Nets suitable for large doors?
Yes, pleated nets are designed for large openings and fold away neatly when not in use.
Do you provide measurement?
Yes, we provide professional on-site measurement. Free within Mysore, outside Mysore ₹15/km travel charge.
Do you provide installation?
Yes, we provide professional installation for all our mosquito net systems.
How long does installation take?
Installation typically takes 1-3 hours depending on the number of windows/doors.
What mesh options are available?
We offer stainless steel (SS) mesh and fibre (Touflex) mesh in various grades and thicknesses.
What is the warranty?
SS Mesh & Pleated: 1-year warranty. Aluminium Frame: 5-year warranty against manufacturing defects.
Do you provide service outside Mysore?
Yes, we serve Mysore, Mandya, Chamrajanagar, Gundlupet, H.D. Kote, Kollegala and nearby areas. Contact us for availability.
How do I get an estimate?
You can use our online estimate form, AI price checker, or contact us directly via call or WhatsApp.
Can I send a photo of my window or door on WhatsApp?
Yes, you can send photos to our WhatsApp number and we’ll provide guidance and a preliminary estimate.
