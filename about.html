Our Story

Raj Marketing Mysore was established in 2016 with a mission to provide high-quality mosquito protection solutions for homes and businesses. Starting from a small workshop in Mysore, we have grown into a trusted name in the mosquito net industry.

We specialize in Velcro nets, Aluminium frame systems, Pleated nets, and custom window screens. Our commitment to quality, craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction has made us the preferred choice for families and businesses across Karnataka.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide innovative, durable, and affordable mosquito protection solutions that enhance the quality of life for every family.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted mosquito net brand in India, known for quality, innovation, and exceptional customer service.
