Frequently Asked Questions
How do I book a measurement?
Click on "Book Measurement" from the dashboard or appointment page. Fill in your details and preferred date. Our team will confirm and visit your location.
What is the delivery time?
Velcro: 2 days. Frame systems: 3 days. Pleated nets: ~3 days. Custom orders may take longer depending on workload and transport.
What warranty do you provide?
SS Mesh & Pleated Nets: 1-year warranty. Aluminium Frame: 5-year warranty. Warranty applies to manufacturing defects only. Physical damage is chargeable.
Do you serve outside Mysore?
Yes, we serve Mysore, Mandya, Chamarajanagar, Gundlupet, H.D. Kote, Kollegala and nearby areas. Outside Mysore: ₹15/km travel charge.
How do I pay?
We accept 60% advance for order confirmation and the balance after completion. Payment methods: Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer.
Can I cancel or modify my order?
Yes, you can cancel or modify within 24 hours of order placement. Post-confirmation cancellations may have deductions as per policy.
How do I track my order?
Go to "My Orders" from your dashboard, or use the Track Order page with your Order ID.
Do you provide free site visits?
Yes, we provide free site visits within Mysore city. Outside Mysore, a travel charge of ₹15/km applies.
